We propose an AI-powered enhancement to this workflow:
When a user finishes filling out and submits an Early Warning record in eRFX, the AI will automatically assess the severity and materiality of the risk information they input, then prompt the submitter with a guided question: Would you like to raise a formal SRM financial review request to Magna Risk Team for further in-depth investigation?

1. AI Real-Time Risk Judgement Triggered Upon Early Warning Submission
Once the user completes all required fields of the Early Warning form (risk description, rumour source, potential financial impact, affected business volume, supplier DUNS & legal name), the embedded LLM AI conducts instant risk evaluation:
AI parses the full text of the warning description, extracting key risk tags such as liquidity crisis, pending litigation, factory shutdown, equity transfer, payment default, mass layoffs, raw material supply disruption etc.
AI cross-references the supplier’s historical Magna financial rating, existing Coface Score and annual spending volume stored in eRFX to calculate the material risk level (High / Medium / Low).
AI sets the pop-up prompt rule automatically: 
- High & Medium risk warnings: Mandatory pop-up prompt asking whether to create an SRM review request;
- Low-risk trivial rumours (e.g. minor temporary logistics delay): AI provides an optional soft reminder without forced pop-up.

2. Auto Pre-Populate SharePoint Request Template & Generate Direct Link
If the submitter selects "Yes" to launch a formal SRM review, AI executes back-end automation instantly:
AI automatically inherits all core supplier identifiers from the Early Warning record: DUNS number, supplier legal name, manufacturing location region, supplier contact details, relevant Magna Division, Buyer contact information, and full risk description text copied from the warning entry.
AI pre-fills all corresponding mandatory fields of the SharePoint New Financial Review Request form, eliminating manual retyping for the submitter.
The system generates a unique, one-click direct access link pointing to this pre-filled SharePoint draft item, and displays the link on the eRFX Early Warning page for the submitter to copy and open immediately.
The link carries embedded supplier risk context from the Early Warning, so the SRM Reviewer will see the full rumour background once they receive the SharePoint notification email.
3. End-to-End Linked Data Synchronization Between eRFX Early Warning & SharePoint Request
a. AI builds a permanent bidirectional association ID to link the eRFX Early Warning record and the matching SharePoint review request: 
- The Early Warning detail page shows a clickable link to jump to the corresponding SharePoint financial review ticket;
- The SharePoint request form auto-attaches a reference to the source eRFX Early Warning entry, with full warning content archived as supporting attachment.
b. Real-time status sync: Once the SRM team completes the Magna financial review and updates the final rating in SharePoint & eRFX, AI pushes the review conclusion back to the original Early Warning record in eRFX, marking the warning as "Resolved" with full risk assessment remarks visible to all eRFX registered users.

4. Supplementary AI Auxiliary Capabilities
AI intelligent risk classification suggestion: While the user types the Early Warning description, the LLM automatically recommends matching risk category tags (Litigation, Liquidity Risk, Operation Suspension, Credit Default etc.) to standardize warning data.
Automated follow-up reminder: If the user closes the prompt without confirming to create a SharePoint request for a High-risk warning, AI sends a follow-up reminder email to the submitter after 24 hours, reiterating the recommendation to initiate an SRM review.
Archive linkage: All Early Warning records and their associated SharePoint review documents are automatically filed into the supplier’s dedicated eRFX archive folder managed by AI, fully traceable for internal audit.
